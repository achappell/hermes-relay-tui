- (decision by user) CAP-1 baseline: use a conformance fixture of at least 20 seconds of 24 kHz mono signed 16-bit PCM spanning at least two Hermes audio segments; producer follows a recorded live trace and consumer drains at real-time rate.
- (decision by user) CAP-1 accounting boundary: source is concatenated Hermes PCM before WAV encoding; delivered audio is bridge PCM after WAV/HTTP framing and before device resampling; compare exact byte counts and SHA-256, with duration calculated at 24 kHz mono signed 16-bit.
- (decision by user) CAP-1/CAP-2 terminal contract: successful EOF requires Hermes completion, all expected PCM delivered, and an empty response queue; pre-header failures use HTTP errors, while post-header failures publish unavailable for the same sequence through a device-visible status path, abort playback, and return the Puck to reusable idle.
- (decision by user) CAP-2 device-visible failure mechanism: expose authenticated GET /response-status?seq=N; bridge records complete or unavailable, Puck checks after media-player idle, unavailable aborts remaining playback and reports locally, and status expires after the next sequence or bounded short TTL without audio or transcript text.
- (decision by user) P-5 queue policy: cap queued PCM at 480,000 bytes (10 source-seconds at 24 kHz mono signed 16-bit), split incoming chunks as needed, apply asynchronous backpressure without drop/replay, and mark unavailable if consumer progress does not resume within the 20-second stall budget.
- (decision by user) P-5 timing contract: for an active sequence, gaps under 20 seconds without successful PCM progress remain recoverable and emit no EOF; at 20 seconds without progress the response becomes unavailable.
