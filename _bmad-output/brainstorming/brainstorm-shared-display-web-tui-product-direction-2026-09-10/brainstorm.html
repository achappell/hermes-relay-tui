06 / MoSCoW

Protect the proving slice.

The larger direction stays alive. The first release earns its way forward by making typed choices and structured selection work across the three surfaces.

Must prove now

  • Hermes emits an interactive choice object.
  • Display, web/iPad, and TUI render native selection and exploration actions.
  • The chosen action returns as structured input.
  • The action remains visible in the session transcript.

Should follow

  • Deeper exploration of a decision object.
  • Calendar controls for dates.
  • Vault Markdown as a readable, discussable artifact.
  • Richer editing with visible diffs and explicit Apply.

Could stay alive

  • A persistent trust strip for session, artifact, and Hermes state.
  • Reversible conversation-focus and artifact-focus modes.
  • An anchored artifact reference when conversation takes focus.

Won’t in first slice

  • Arbitrary UI.
  • Unreviewed autonomous changes.
  • A generic card for every semantic interaction.
