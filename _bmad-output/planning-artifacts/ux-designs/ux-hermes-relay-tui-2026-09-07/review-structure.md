Compared with the earlier reports, the current pair has incorporated the FR crosswalk, priority and persistence rules, state-presentation mapping, per-surface state matrix, privacy lifecycle, and explicit visual-history ownership. The findings below narrow the remaining structural work and preserve those gains.
